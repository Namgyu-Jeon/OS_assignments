# Dependency and Supply-Chain Playbook

## Required research

Before installing or editing locks, verify from primary sources:

- exact name, version, platform, and architecture;
- official source and immutable download reference;
- checksum, signature, key fingerprint, or documented trust path;
- license, transitive components, redistribution obligations, and notices;
- maintenance and security status;
- build and runtime impact;
- simpler alternatives.

## Approval proposal

State changed manifests and locks, artifact locations, license conclusion, verification commands, rollback, and unresolved risk. A development-only dependency must still be classified and justified.

## Stop conditions

Do not improvise with an arbitrary mirror, latest URL, third-party binary, disabled signature check, weakened hash, or broader license profile. Preserve source/provenance evidence required by the approved distribution policy.
